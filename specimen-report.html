1. The reading

The vendor's claim for this deployment is 99.0% pick success on in-scope SKUs. Over the window the deployment logged 41,206 in-scope pick attempts. We scored a stratified sample of 812.

The claim is not supported. 99.0% lies outside the interval. The measured rate is 5.6 points below the claim. Against the pre-registered threshold of 90.0% for this site, the deployment passes and the grade is Current.

4. Our error on this report

160 of the 812 sampled picks (20%, stratified by class) were independently scored by a human who did not see the automatic result. Agreement: κ = 0.86. Disagreements: 9. The scorer's measured misclassification rates were used to correct the estimate and are included in the interval above.

Size
