    The finding

    Training it more times won't stop you shipping a broken skill.

    Retrain eight times, ship the best validation score. That is current best practice. Here is how often it still ships a skill that got worse.

    Train once, ship itNo selection possible
    11–79%
    Train 8×, ship the best-validation oneWhat labs do today
    3–93%
    Train 3×, pick per skill, abstain when unsureThree retrains, not eight
    2–14%

    More candidates don't help. In one cell, best-validation selection got worse with more of them. What helps is choosing per skill, and holding the incumbent when the evidence is thin.

    The research

    We publish the results that went against us.

    Every wave is pre-registered, decision bands frozen before any data is scored. When a claim fails, it ships as a failure. 701 policies so far.

    Our routing result gained +5.6 points on one suite. We ran it on a second suite and got +1.7, with zero inside the interval. So the gain is suite-dependent — and we say so in the paper, on this page, and in the tool's own output.
