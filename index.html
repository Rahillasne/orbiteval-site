What we do

What nobody else in the loop can do.

Count

We re-score what the robot already recorded.

A robot's dashboard reports its own success. We draw a sample of its picks from the video and logs it already keeps, score them against a rubric written down before we look, and report the rate with an honest interval. No cameras to install, no model to hand over.

Publish our error

Every report says how often we're wrong.

A human re-scores a fixed fraction of every sample. The disagreement rate is printed on the report and folded into the interval. If you can't see a verifier's error rate, you can't use their number.

Keep checking

An acceptance test happens once. We don't stop.

Item mixes drift, models get updated, and the contract number is never measured again. We re-check monthly. A grade that stops being true is revoked, in public, and the history stays up.

How an audit works

Four steps, in this order, every time.

Pre-register

The rubric, sampling rule, sample size, and threshold are written, hashed, and timestamped before any data is pulled. The hash is on the grade page.

Sample

We choose the site and the window. Picks are drawn from the logs by a published seed, stratified by item class, so anyone can regenerate exactly the sample we scored.

Score and calibrate

An automatic scorer reads each pick. A human double-scores at least a fifth of them. Their disagreement goes into the interval, not into a footnote.

Publish and re-check

The grade goes on a public page you can link to. It is re-checked monthly with a fresh seed, and revoked if it stops holding.

The full procedure, including the rubric and the statistics, is on the method page. The interval and sizing code is open.

Independence

How we stay independent.

Conflict policy · in force from the first audit

  1. We take no equity, options, or warrants from any robot vendor.
  2. We choose the site and the time window. A vendor never picks the sample.
  3. A vendor chooses whether to be audited, never which results are shown. Every audited deployment is listed in the registry.
  4. Grades are revocable, and revocations are public.
  5. We never delete history. A revoked grade stays visible, marked revoked.

What you receive

A report, not a dashboard.

The deliverable is a document. It opens with the gap between the claimed rate and the measured one, breaks the failures down by item class so you know what to fix, states our own error rate, and links every pick we scored so you can check us.
