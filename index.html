How we stay independent.

The conflict policy, in force from the first audit. It is versioned with the method.

  1. We take no equity, options, or warrants from any robot vendor.
  2. We choose the site and the time window. A vendor never picks the sample.
  3. A vendor chooses whether to be audited, never which results are shown. Every audited deployment is listed in the registry.
  4. Grades are revocable, and revocations are public.
  5. We never delete history. A revoked grade stays visible, marked revoked.

Questions & answers

Do you install cameras or sensors?

No. We score the pick video and logs the deployment already keeps. Independence comes from the procedure: pre-registration, a published seed, a published error rate and a revocable grade, not from owning the sensor.

Who pays for an audit?

The party making the claim, first: the robot vendor. Like a safety mark, the maker pays for the test and the buyer gets a result they didn't have to run themselves. Operators and insurers can commission an audit too.

Can a vendor hide a bad result?

No. A vendor chooses whether to be audited, never which results are shown. Every audited deployment is listed in the registry, and a revoked grade stays visible, marked revoked, with the reason in one sentence.

Is this a leaderboard?

No. One page per deployment, one status, its own history. Deployments are never ranked against each other, because the item mix, the scope and the threshold differ at every site.

What if there isn't enough data?

The report says so. If the window can't deliver the pre-registered precision, the status is Insufficient data and the report states what sample would resolve it. We don't round that up to a verdict.

Do you need our model or training data?

Never. The method needs the pick log, the video the operator already holds, and the in-scope SKU list. We hold no weights and no training data.

How fast is this live?

One thirty-minute call. Pre-registration inside a week. The first report follows the first four complete weeks of the window, and re-checks are monthly after that.
