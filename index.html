The finding

Training it more times doesn't stop you shipping a broken skill.

Retrain a policy eight times and ship the one with the best validation score. That's current best practice. Here's how often it still ships a skill that regressed.

What you doShips a damaged skill
Train once, ship itNo selection possible 11–79%
Train 8×, ship the best-validation oneWhat labs do today 3–93%
Train 3×, select per skill, abstain when unsureWhat orbit-eval does 2–14%

More candidates don't help — in one cell, best-validation selection got worse with more of them. What helps is choosing per skill and holding the incumbent when the evidence is thin. At ten skills, three retrains is enough. At fifty, it isn't — so the budget has to be sized, not guessed.

The research

We publish the results that went against us.

Every wave is pre-registered with its decision bands frozen before any data is scored. When a claim fails, it ships as a failure.

Our routing result gained +5.6 points on one suite. We tested it on a second suite and got +1.7, with zero inside the confidence interval. So the gain is suite-dependent, and we say so — in the paper, on this page, and in the tool's own output.
