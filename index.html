How we stay independent.

The conflict policy. Versioned with the method.

  1. We take no equity, options, or warrants from any robot vendor.
  2. One record per release pair and claim. Three claims get three records, never one blended verdict.
  3. Missing information degrades a verdict and never raises one.
  4. A study that could not have detected the claimed effect is insufficient evidence, never supported.
  5. When our own tool is wrong, we say so in public. Its first version called five of twenty published claims supported. None survive.

Questions

Do you install anything?

No. We read the evidence you already keep: evaluation logs, per-episode results, incident records.

Who pays?

The vendor, first. Like a safety mark: the maker pays for the test, the buyer gets the result.

Will you call a claim false?

No. A claim the evidence cannot reach is insufficient evidence, not a refuted claim.

Is it a leaderboard?

No. One record per release pair and claim, never ranked against another.

What if the evidence is thin?

The record says so, and says whether more of the same kind of evidence could ever settle it.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Is it real? runs in your browser, and the Release Record runs on two real sources.
