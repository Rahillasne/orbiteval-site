How we stay independent.

The conflict policy. Versioned with the method.

  1. We take no equity, options, or warrants from any robot vendor.
  2. We choose the site and the time window. A vendor never picks the sample.
  3. A vendor chooses whether to be audited, never which results are shown. Every audited deployment is in the registry.
  4. Grades are revocable, and revocations are public.
  5. We never delete history. A revoked grade stays visible, marked revoked.

Questions

Do you install anything?

No. We score the video and logs the site already keeps.

Who pays?

The vendor, first. Like a safety mark: the maker pays for the test, the buyer gets the result.

Can a vendor hide a bad result?

No. Choosing to be audited is the vendor's call. What gets shown is not.

Is it a leaderboard?

No. One page per deployment, never ranked against another.

What if there isn't enough data?

The status says Insufficient data. We don't round it up to a verdict.

How fast?

One call. Pre-registration within a week. First report after four weeks of data, then monthly.
