Calibration · our error rate

How often we're wrong.

Every number comes from a scorer, and every scorer makes mistakes. Ours are published here.

First calibration study in progress

We are double-scoring public piece-picking footage, model and human independently. No report is issued before the first figures exist, and no agreement figure appears on this site until then.

What will appear

Overall agreement. Cohen's κ between model and human, with a 95% interval and the number of picks it rests on.

Confusion matrix. For each rubric outcome, where model and human disagreed and what each said.

By item class. Rigid, bagged, deformable, transparent or reflective: where scorers fail.

By scorer version. Every version, dated, with its own figures. Never edited in place.

How error enters a report

Measured misclassification rates correct the point estimate and widen the interval. A weaker scorer never moves a number in its own favour. Below the protocol's agreement threshold, a class is reported as Insufficient data, not as a number.

Human scorers

They see the rubric and the pick, never the model's answer, and are paid per pick regardless of outcome. Their own agreement is measured on a shared subset and published too.
