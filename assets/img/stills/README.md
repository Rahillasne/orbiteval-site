# Stills

Real-time renders of the R2 / S2 CAD models (three.js, `film.html?mode=still`). They are not photographs; caption them as renders on the site.
2400×1500 JPG (q85) + WebP of each. Re-render all of them with `node tools/stills.mjs` (local server on :8811).

- `r2-hero.jpg`: R2 from three-quarter front-left, eyes on, parcels visible through the smoked service-side shutter.
- `r2-front.jpg`: R2 almost straight on and centered; a 4:5 center crop works (3D viewer poster).
- `r2-rack.jpg`: The service side, with trays and parcels behind the smoked shutter.
- `station.jpg`: The S2 drop-off station. The screen (headed "RARI S2", from BRAND in config.js) reads "1104 · Apartment found", the courier window halo is lit, and R2 is docked in its bay.
- `finger-press.jpg`: R2's finger pod pressing the elevator call button, with the ring lit.
- `fob-tap.jpg`: R2's pod tapping the building's fob reader under the car panel. The reader ring turns green.
- `handoff.jpg`: The tray out at counter height at apartment 1104, with warm light from inside the open door.
- `tower.jpg`: A holographic building with 23 floors, the elevator shaft and robots on the floors. Warm dots are deliveries coming in from the front and back entrances and riding the shaft up to the floors.
- `r2-cutout.png`: R2 alone on a transparent background, 1600×2000, three-quarter front-left.
