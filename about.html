About OrbitEval

OrbitEval builds ship-decision infrastructure for learned robot policies — software that measures the retrain noise floor of a robot-learning stack and prices every ship/no-ship decision against it, so a real improvement is provable and a lucky training seed never reaches a fleet.

What we build

One engine. Three surfaces.

OrbitEval sits on top of the eval harness a robotics team already runs and turns what they already log into decisions they can defend.

01

Promotion gate

A CI status check that sits between the eval harness and deployment. Before a retrained checkpoint ships, it rules SHIP / HOLD / COLLECT-MORE / INVALID / UNRESOLVABLE with quantified error rates.

commercial · drops into CI
02

Acceptance clauses

Pre-registered, machine-checkable statistical clauses over fleet telemetry, tested with anytime-valid sequential statistics — so a pilot or milestone settles itself the moment the evidence suffices.

commercial · contract-grade
03

Certificate ledger

Append-only, tamper-evident decision records you can hand a customer, an insurer or an auditor — every verdict lands with its full config and statistics attached.

commercial · signed evidence

The measurement behind it

Built on the Variance Atlas.

The product is priced against a measurement we made and published: the Variance Atlas — 16 pre-registered noise-floor cells across four policy classes (ACT, Diffusion Policy, SmolVLA, π₀.₅), measured from 382 controlled training runs. Every result behind the product was pre-registered before scoring.

The atlas is free and public — every measured cell is published at the Variance Atlas.
The method is written up — how the noise floor is measured, at The Noise Floor.
The free check runs on the landing page — price a ship decision against a measured cell in the noise-floor check, no account needed.
