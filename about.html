About OrbitEval

OrbitEval is the statistical release layer for robot policies. It takes the evaluation logs a robotics team already produces and says, with priced uncertainty, which skills the new model broke and what the run cannot tell them.

What exists today

Three things, two of them free

The same statistics run underneath all three. What differs is whose evaluation logs they run on.

orbit-eval

Free · Apache-2.0 
$ pip install orbit-eval

A command-line tool with zero dependencies. orbit-eval release evals.csv reports which skills regressed between two releases, priced against noise, and exits 1 on a regression so it drops into CI.

The measurement behind it

Calibrated on replicate retrainings

The statistics are calibrated on the only published replicate-retraining corpus in robot imitation learning: 701 controlled retrainings across ACT, Diffusion Policy, SmolVLA and π0.5, every wave pre-registered before scoring. The measured noise floors are published as the Variance Atlas, and the method is written up in how the noise floor is measured.

Independence

Not affiliated with any benchmark

OrbitEval is not affiliated with RoboChallenge, RoboDojo, WBench, LeRobot or any benchmark. Scores on the arena are the benchmarks' own. What is added is the capability cut and the uncertainty they do not publish, and every figure that can be checked against a benchmark's published number is.
