What a grade page shows

One deployment, one page, the whole history.

The reading

Claimed, measured, interval.

The same block that opens every report: the claimed rate, the measured one, and the 95% interval drawn on an axis so the gap is visible at a glance.

The status

Current, insufficient data, or revoked.

With the date of the last re-check, the date the next one is due, and — for a revocation — the reason, in one sentence.

The record

Every re-check, in order.

Protocol hash, seed, sample size, agreement, and the interval for each month the grade has existed. Nothing is ever removed from this list.
